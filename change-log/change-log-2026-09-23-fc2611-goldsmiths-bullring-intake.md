## 2026-09-23 (late morning) — a new project, and Anna's first work outside the estate's own property

Minda put four documents into `Raw/` and asked Anna to read them. They are a **client fit-out tender**
— Watches of Switzerland Group, Goldsmiths at 230 Bullring, Birmingham — not an estate property. He
then issued the project code **FC2611**, which puts it under **Fishbone Construction Ltd**, and
confirmed the working location: the Fishbone Construction projects folder.

**What was read.** All four, in full: Zebra Projects' 42290 detailed design package (49,920 characters
of extracted text, deduplicated and worked through in sections), Breitling SA's own brand drawing set,
Macdonald Joinery's hoarding set-out, and the blank valuation sheet.

**The finding that matters.** Zebra's drawings require the hoarding to be **1 hour fire rated with a
Class 0 surface spread of flame on both sides**, with **FD60 fire double doors for means of escape**.
The quotation prices it **non-fire-rated 9 mm MDF**, twice — item 2.02 and again item 3.05 for the
December re-visit. Macdonald's drawing carries no fire note at all. And quote item 2.04, the Rolex CPO
hoarding door, is marked **OMITTED** where Zebra's demolition plan shows an FD60 fire door for means
of escape.

Three documents, one specification, and only the designer's copy asks for fire rating. In a shopping
centre that is usually a condition of getting on site.

**Three further conflicts**, each of which moves the price: Breitling's own pack gives the unit as
**30.5 m²** against Zebra's **27.65 m²** and the quote's 28 m² of tiling; the brick slips are
attributed three different ways (Craftstone supply, "supplied and installed by Breitling", and free
issue install-only "By Scoppio"); and Breitling's **CE01 open ceiling spray painted RAL 9004** is a
different operation from the emulsion decoration the quote prices.

**Five defects inside the quotation itself**: item number 2.13 used twice; three `#VALUE!` errors and
a whole `#REF!` valuation column; a rate reading "By Others?" with a question mark; drawing cited as
**12290**/106 when Zebra's project is **42290**; and a filename date that disagrees with the sheet
header.

**Confidentiality checked before filing, not after.** Breitling's pack carries an express restriction
— confidential, Breitling SA's intellectual property, not to be transmitted to third parties without
written permission. Zebra's sheets bar disclosure to unauthorised persons. So the destination's
sharing was read first: `Fishbone Construction / C Projects` is **domain-only** —
`fishboneconstruction.co.uk` as writer plus the owner, nothing else. No consumer mailboxes, no outside
domains. That is the project team, which is who the drawings are issued to. Had these gone to the
Commercial Properties folder they would have reached **seven writers including two consumer
mailboxes** (AQ-11). Worth recording as the first time that access review paid for itself.
